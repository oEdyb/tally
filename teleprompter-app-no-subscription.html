A teleprompter app for iPhone with no subscription

Tally is an iPhone teleprompter you pay for once. Download it free, try the built-in sample, then unlock your own scripts and video recording with a single purchase (US $19.99, Apple shows your local price). No subscription, no timed trial that turns into a charge, no account.

What is free and what you pay for

  • Free: the app and a built-in English sample script, so you can hear how the text follows your voice before you buy. The sample does not record video.
  • One-time unlock: write, paste or import your own scripts, and record video takes with the front camera.
  • Not charged: no weekly, monthly or yearly fee, and no renewal to cancel.

How it works

  1. Install Tally and open Try free sample. Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition.
  2. Read the sample out loud. The text follows your voice.
  3. If it suits you, buy the unlock once in the app. Restore Purchases brings it back on another iPhone signed in with the same Apple Account.
  4. Add your script, tap Rehearse, record, then save the take to Photos or share it.

How the price compares

Many teleprompter apps charge by the month or year. On 2026-10-20, the Teleprompter.com pricing page listed Pro at $19.99 per month, or $7.50 per month billed yearly. One month at the monthly rate costs the same as Tally's one-time unlock. The Teleprompter for Video and PromptSmart+ US App Store listings show monthly and annual in-app subscriptions.

What a one-time price does not include

  • Tally is for iPhone and needs iOS 26 or later.
  • Tally records and saves takes. It has no built-in video editor or captions, so editing happens in Photos or the editor you already use.
  • It imports TXT, Markdown, RTF and PDFs with selectable text. It does not import Word (.docx) files, so paste the text or save the document as RTF first.
  • Voice following can lag or lose your place. You can drag the text to pause and tap a paragraph to set your place, or scroll manually.

Questions

Is Tally really free?

The download and the built-in sample are free. Your own scripts and video recording need the one-time unlock.

Is there a subscription?

No. The unlock is one non-consumable in-app purchase. Nothing renews.

Is there a free trial that charges me later?

No. There is no timed trial. The free sample stays available as long as you like, so you can decide before you pay.

Do I need an account?

No. Tally has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your scripts and recordings stay on your iPhone.

What if I get a new iPhone?

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

Does Tally add a watermark to my videos?

No. Takes are saved without a watermark.

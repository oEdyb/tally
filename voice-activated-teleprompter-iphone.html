A voice-activated teleprompter for iPhone

Tally is a voice-activated teleprompter for iPhone. It listens through the microphone, uses Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone to find your place in the script, and scrolls the text to match, so you read at your own pace instead of a fixed speed. Try it free with the built-in sample.

Get Tally on the App Store

How it works

  1. Open Try free sample, or open your script and tap Prepare.
  2. Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition. If asked, install the speech files for your language.
  3. Turn on Follow speech and start reading. The text moves to the line you are speaking.
  4. If you lose your place, drag the text to pause and tap the paragraph you are on. Manual scrolling is always available.

Set up for eye contact

  • Move the reading line near the front camera so you look at the lens while you read.
  • Change text size and width, and turn on mirror mode if you use a glass rig.
  • Use the countdown to get ready before the take starts.

Limits to know

  • Voice following needs Microphone and Speech permission, a supported language and device, and installed speech files. Downloading those files needs an internet connection.
  • Recognition can lag or lose your place. That is why manual scrolling and tap-to-set-place stay available.
  • The free sample is in English and does not record video. Recording your own scripts needs the one-time unlock.
  • Requires an iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

Privacy

Your voice is processed by Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone. Audio is not sent to us, and Tally has no accounts or analytics. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

What does voice-activated mean in Tally?

The script follows where you are in the text as you speak it, instead of scrolling at a fixed speed. If you read faster or slower, the text keeps up.

Does it need an internet connection?

Speech recognition runs on your iPhone. Your iPhone may need to download speech language files from Apple the first time you use a language, and that needs a connection.

Which languages work?

Voice following depends on the languages Apple's speech recognition supports on your iPhone. Pick the script's language in Prepare and check that its speech files are installed. The built-in sample is English.

What if it loses my place?

Drag the text to pause, then tap the paragraph you are reading. You can also switch to manual scrolling.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The free sample uses the same voice following. Your own scripts and recording are a one-time unlock, with no subscription.

Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026.

Who we are

Tally is published by Dyberg & Co AB, Sweden. Privacy questions: support@dyberg.co.

Your scripts and recordings

Tally has no accounts and no server. It stores your scripts, settings and recordings on your iPhone. We don't upload them and we can't see them. Your device backups may include them, depending on your iCloud or computer backup settings.

Camera, microphone and speech

Tally uses the camera and microphone to record your takes. It uses Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone to follow your place in the script. The audio is not sent to us. Your iPhone may download speech language files from Apple. You can turn off these permissions at any time in Settings.

Photos and sharing

When you tap Save to Photos, Tally asks for permission to add the video to your photo library. It never reads your photo library. When you share a recording, the app or service you choose handles that copy under its own policy.

Purchases

Apple processes the in-app purchase. Tally only checks Apple's signed purchase record to unlock features. We never receive your payment details.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Tally contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.

Support email

If you email us, we receive what you choose to send and use it only to answer you. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

Deleting your data

Delete scripts and takes in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Copies you saved to Photos or shared elsewhere stay where you put them.

Children

Tally doesn't knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Tally's data practices change, we'll update this page and its effective date.

Where the others do more

  • PromptSmart+ lists voice following in 15 languages. Tally's voice following works in the languages Apple's on-device speech recognition supports on your iPhone, once the speech files are installed. The free sample is English.
  • PromptSmart and Teleprompter for Video import Word files. Tally does not.
  • Teleprompter for Video lists in-app editing, captions and remote control. Tally records and saves takes and leaves editing to Photos or your own editor.
  • Teleprompter.com runs on Android, Mac and the web. Tally is iPhone only, iOS 26 or later.

Where Tally differs

  • One price: a single unlock instead of a subscription or add-on tiers.

  • Try before you pay: the real voice following works in the free sample.
  • Take-focused: record separate takes, review them in the app, and keep a take if a call interrupts the recording.

Questions

Is Tally affiliated with PromptSmart?

No. Tally is an independent app from a different publisher. PromptSmart is a trademark of its owner.

Is PromptSmart subscription-only?

No. On the US App Store, PromptSmart Pro is a $29.99 purchase with optional in-app purchases, while PromptSmart+ is a free download that needs a subscription for its core features.

Can I bring my scripts over?

Yes. Tally imports TXT, Markdown, RTF and PDFs with selectable text, and you can paste text. It does not import .docx, so save a Word document as RTF or paste the text.

Does Tally have a free trial?

It has a free sample instead of a timed trial. You can try voice following with the sample before you buy. Your own scripts and recording need the one-time unlock.

Does Tally work on iPad or Android?

No. Tally is for iPhone and needs iOS 26 or later.

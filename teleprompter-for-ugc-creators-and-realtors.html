A teleprompter for UGC creators and real estate agents

Tally is an iPhone teleprompter for people who record a script to camera over and over: UGC creators filming brand scripts and real estate agents filming listing and market updates. The text follows your voice beside the front camera, you record several takes, and you save the best one to Photos. One purchase, no subscription.

A workflow for repeat scripts

  1. Paste the script or import it as TXT, Markdown, RTF or a PDF with selectable text.
  2. In Prepare, move the reading line near the camera and set the text size.
  3. Tap Rehearse and record. The text follows your voice, so a stumble does not leave you behind.
  4. Record more takes of the same script. Review them in Takes.
  5. Save the one you want to Photos, or share it, and edit it in the app you already use.

For UGC creators

  • Brands often send a script or brief as a document. Tally does not import Word (.docx), so paste the text or save it as RTF first.
  • Keep one script per brief in the library, and record separate takes for hook, body and call to action.
  • No watermark is added to your videos, and nothing is uploaded, which matters when a brief is under embargo.

For real estate agents

  • Keep reusable scripts for listing intros, open-house invitations and market updates, then change the numbers each time.
  • Read to the front camera at your own pace without memorizing, and keep eye contact with the lens.
  • Mirror mode is there if you film through a glass teleprompter rig.

Limits to know

  • Tally has no built-in editor, captions or cloud sync. It records and saves takes.
  • Voice following can lag or lose your place. Drag the text to pause and tap the paragraph to continue.
  • iPhone only, iOS 26 or later.

Questions

Can I record several takes of the same script?

Yes. Each recording is saved as a separate take that you can play back in Takes, then save to Photos or share.

Can I use a script a client sent me?

Paste the text, or import it as TXT, Markdown, RTF or a PDF with selectable text. Word (.docx) files are not supported, so save them as RTF first.

What happens if a call interrupts a recording?

Tally keeps the take and marks it as recovered. Play it to check it before you use it.

Can I see my script next to the camera?

Yes. Set the reading position near the camera in Prepare so your eyes stay close to the lens.

Does it cost a monthly fee?

No. The free download includes a sample. Your own scripts and recording are one purchase, with no subscription.

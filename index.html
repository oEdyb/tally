Tally Support

Tally is a teleprompter for iPhone. The text follows your voice while you record.

Contact

Email support@dyberg.co. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, the Tally version and a short description of the problem. Don't send private scripts or recordings unless you want to share them for support.

Common questions

  • The text doesn't follow my voice. Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Tally in Settings. Check that the script's language is selected in Prepare and its speech files are installed. Turn on Follow speech before you tap Rehearse.
  • I lost my place. Drag the text to pause, then tap the paragraph you're reading.
  • Recording doesn't start. Allow Camera and Microphone for Tally in Settings, and check that your iPhone has free storage.
  • I bought the unlock on another iPhone. Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  • Saving a video. Open Takes, select a recording, then tap Save to Photos or Share.
  • A take says it was recovered. Tally keeps a take when a recording is interrupted, for example by a phone call. Play it to check it before you use it.
